### Public work

Most of what I work on is private — client code, and my own products.
These are the ones I can show:

**[coreText](https://github.com/mishanyaeveryday/coreText)** — Ctrl+F by meaning
Chrome extension that finds passages by what they mean, not by exact words. Type
`romeo dies`, land on *"Thus with a kiss I die."* Pages over 400k characters are
indexed through Gemini File Search, with the index dropped when the tab closes.
`TypeScript` · `Gemini API` · `Chrome Extensions`
*Built at a Google AI hackathon, Sep 2026*

**[Placebo Coach](https://github.com/mishanyaeveryday/hackathon)** — N=1 experiments on yourself
A platform for running placebo-controlled trials on your own wellness habits:
randomized slots, 30% of them placebo, blinded until the experiment ends, then
effectiveness compared against the control. Django REST backend, React frontend,
three languages.
`Django` · `DRF` · `PostgreSQL` · `React` · `TypeScript` · `JWT`

**[Shadowsteam](https://github.com/Unity-PM/PSM_s2_L01_Shadowsteam)** — Unity game
Built with a team of four at university.
`Unity` · `C#`
